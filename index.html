LAN · AUTHENTICATED

Organise work without replacing Hermes

Office creates a separate Kanban board for each team and registers a removable, on-demand cooperation skill for each member. It leaves each profile’s base identity and SOUL.md untouched. Disbanding archives the board and moves only Office’s exact team skills out of Hermes’ active skill folders.

⚠Scope: Kanban access is enabled per profile and platform, not restricted to a single board. Office guidance routes relevant work to the selected board, but this is not a security boundary. The team skill is on-demand; load it for that team’s work. The app does not alter credentials, A2A permissions, other toolsets, or the agents’ existing creative/role restrictions. Team activation does not restart the Hermes gateway.
